## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-ticket prestige, intellectual rigor, and bespoke literary craftsmanship tailored for elite ghostwriting and executive publishing. It bridges old-world European editorial legacy with modern executive clarity. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Editorial Luxury and High-End Minimalism**. Visual weight is driven by substantial typographic hierarchy, generous breathing room, rich pigment-dense burgundies, tactile warm off-white canvases, and delicate antique gold accents. Interfaces convey discretion, timeless authority, and uncompromising refinement.

## Colors

The palette balances pigment, ink, and raw paper texture:

- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The primary background is `canvas-off-white` (`#FAF7F2`), paired with `surface-cream` (`#F2ECE2`) for container grouping, cards, and quiet section alternation. Pure `#FFFFFF` is restricted to high-contrast document sheets or crisp callout elements.
- **Brand Pigments**: `bordeaux-deep` (`#6B1F2E`) serves as the primary brand color for critical call-to-actions, focused selections, and signature framing. `bordeaux-wine` (`#4A1520`) acts as the darker tier for deep contrast states, footer zones, and executive headers.
- **Accents & Borders**: `aged-gold` (`#B8935A`) provides metallic poise for fine rules, crest borders, insignia badges, and premium status highlights. `border-subtle` (`#8B3A47`) handles hairline borders on deep tonal elements.
- **Typography & Neutrals**: Primary typography utilizes `charcoal-ink` (`#2B2424`) to deliver softer reading contrast than stark black. Secondary editorial credits, captions, and structural meta-information use `warm-gray` (`#6B5E5E`).

## Typography

The typographical engine pairs the classical Didone warmth of **Playfair Display** with the functional clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**:

- **Playfair Display**: Assigned to display headlines, chapter headers, testimonial quotes, and monumental figures. It conveys the tactile pedigree of bespoke book-making.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Drives interface controls, structural metadata, executive summaries, forms, and dense manuscript reading.
- **Eyebrows & Micro-Headers**: Set exclusively in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.15em`), establishing editorial sub-sections reminiscent of classical book chapter headers.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to a disciplined 12-column editorial grid on desktop and 4 columns on mobile, echoing book spreads and broadsheet layouts:

- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Ample vertical breathing space (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) separates thematic sections, avoiding clutter and projecting executive composure.
- **Asymmetric Offsets**: Pull quotes, testimonial excerpts, and manuscript previews often offset by 1 column to emulate luxury editorial margins.
- **Breakpoints**: 
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): Fluid single-column layout, compact margins (`1.5rem`), gutters collapsed to `1rem`.
  - Tablet (`768px – 1024px`): 8-column layout, balanced paddings (`2.5rem`).
  - Desktop (`> 1024px`): 12-column layout capped at `1360px` max-width to preserve ideal typographic measure (65–75 characters per line for body copy).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated tech dropshadows, leaning instead on physical paper stratification, delicate metallic boundaries, and directional lighting:

- **Tonal Stratification**: Depth is primarily established by alternating the base `#FAF7F2` canvas with layered `#F2ECE2` containers, outlined by fine `1px` borders in `border-subtle` (`rgba(139, 58, 71, 0.15)`) or muted gold (`rgba(184, 147, 90, 0.3)`).
- **Executive Editorial Shadow**: For floating dialogs, confidential project dossiers, and key CTA cards, depth uses a soft, multi-layered warm ambient shadow:
  - Low Elevation: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 36, 36, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(43, 36, 36, 0.03);`
  - High Elevation (Floating Folios & Dossiers): `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(43, 36, 36, 0.09), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(43, 36, 36, 0.04);`
- **Gold Linework**: Polished 1px borders in `aged-gold` (`#B8935A`) create a gilded finish on featured tiers without relying on drop shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** posture with controlled, tailored edges:

- Default buttons, inputs, and card containers use subtle rounding (`0.25rem` / `4px`), referencing the bound edges of hardback volumes and archival stationery.
- Executive badges, metadata pills, and micro-tags utilize full pill profiles (`9999px`) to create optical variety against rectangular text containers.
- Featured manuscript and media cards retain crisp structural corners (`0.375rem` / `6px`) framed with delicate gold or burgundy hairpins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Executive Master)**: Background in `bordeaux-deep` (`#6B1F2E`), text in `#FAF7F2`, `4px` border radius, subtle hover transition transitioning to `bordeaux-wine` (`#4A1520`). Includes a faint gold inner focus ring.
- **Secondary (Heritage Outline)**: 1px border in `aged-gold` (`#B8935A`), transparent background, text in `bordeaux-deep` (`#6B1F2E`). Hover fills with `rgba(184, 147, 90, 0.08)`.
- **Text Action**: Monoline link with wide uppercase typography (`label-eyebrow`), accompanied by an arrow glyph (`→`) with a bottom border underline spaced `4px` below.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Built with `surface-cream` (`#F2ECE2`) fill against the `canvas-off-white` (`#FAF7F2`) canvas.
- Perimeter bound by a 1px border in `rgba(107, 31, 46, 0.1)`.
- Top borders for VIP advisory tiers feature a refined `2px` accent line in `aged-gold` (`#B8935A`).

### Form Elements & Text Fields
- Clean underline or soft boxed inputs (`surface-cream` background with a 1px `warm-gray` border at 20% opacity).
- Focused state transitions border to `bordeaux-deep` (`#6B1F2E`) with zero fuzzy glow, complemented by floating labels in uppercase `label-eyebrow`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill geometry with `0.75rem` vertical and `0.25rem` horizontal padding.
- Prestigious ghostwrite status indicators utilize `aged-gold` (`#B8935A`) at 12% opacity fill with sharp `aged-gold` typographic fills.

### Quotations & Manuscript Previews
- Left-bordered with a `2px` solid stroke in `aged-gold` (`#B8935A`).
- Set in italicized `headline-sm` with enlarged serif quotation marks (`Playfair Display`) in `bordeaux-deep` at 30% opacity.
